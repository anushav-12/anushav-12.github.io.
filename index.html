Customer Segmentation with RFM

A segmentation case study on the UCI Online Retail dataset (406,829 orders), built to read like an analyst's answer to a business question rather than a script's output.

Question

Which customers are worth retaining, and who's already drifting away?

Approach

Recency, frequency and monetary scores via SQL window functions (NTILE), plus cohort and retention analysis.

Findings

A small share of customers drive most of revenue; a churn-risk flag surfaces who's slipping out of that group.

Recommendation

An interactive Power BI dashboard with slicers, so the business can act on the segments directly.
